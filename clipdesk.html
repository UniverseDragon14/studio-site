How it works

The service stays manual and controlled until real customer demand proves what should be automated.

Send the brief

Provide your goal, language, preferred platform and a private cloud-storage link.

Confirm scope

You receive a clear deliverable, price and expected turnaround before work begins.

Edit and review

The clip is processed with Universal Dragon Studio tools and reviewed manually.

Private delivery

Finished files and creator notes are delivered through a private share link.

Clear boundaries

No vague “AI magic.” Just boring, useful rules that prevent expensive disasters.

Rights and permission

Only submit footage you own or have permission to use. Copyrighted media cannot be copied, republished or edited without authorization.

Privacy and deletion

Customer links and files are kept private, used only for the agreed project and deleted after the agreed retention period.

Payment and revisions

Scope, lawful payment method, advance amount and included revisions are confirmed before editing begins.

No automatic posting

UD ClipDesk prepares and delivers files. It does not publish to customer accounts or access social-media passwords.
